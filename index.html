Inpulse LMS Problem Statement

Small Construction Contractors Struggle to Track Projects, Materials, Labour and Costs, Leading to Delays and Cost Overruns

Small and mid-sized contractors in the construction industry run high-risk operations relying on fragmented paper logs, messy WhatsApp chats, and disconnected spreadsheets. The office is blind to field realities, resulting in devastating budget leakages.

View Official Problem Statement on Inpulse LMS
01

Ghost Labour & Shift Leakage

Paper muster rolls lead to unverified contractor headcounts, inaccurate overtime, and zero accountability for on-site hours.

02

Material Pilferage & Wastage

Steel, cement, and aggregates arrive on-site with manual chits. Untracked deliveries cause duplicate orders and high scrap percentages.

03

Cascading Milestone Delays

Foundation delays or delayed curing are noticed days later because site engineers fail to report roadblocks promptly.

04

Blind Budget Overruns

Actual site expenses are calculated weeks after work ends. Contractors realize they lost profits only after client handover.
